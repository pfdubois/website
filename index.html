About The Book

"Bridge Bidding" is a free book written for Contract Bridge players whose ability ranges from advanced beginner to advanced. The book has an open-source license.

About Paul

Paul F. Dubois pioneered scientific computing techniques, including computational steering, the Basis System, Numerical Python, and the use of object technology for scientific programming. He retired from Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory in 2005.

Paul is a Ruby Life Master in the American Contract Bridge League, Unit 539 (San Diego).
